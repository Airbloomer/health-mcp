## Developer Certificate of Origin

Every commit must include a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off.

Add the sign-off when you commit:

```bash
git commit -s
```

Git appends a `Signed-off-by` trailer with the committer's name and email. That trailer is the sign-off. A commit without it will not be accepted.

The sign-off certifies that you can submit the contribution under the MIT License:

- You created the contribution, in whole or in part, and you have the right to submit it under the MIT License; or
- The contribution is based on previous work that, to the best of your knowledge, is covered by an appropriate open source license, and you have the right to submit that work, with or without modification, under the MIT License; or
- The contribution was provided to you by someone who certified these terms, and you have not modified it.

You understand that the contribution is public, and that a record of the contribution, including the sign-off, is kept and may be redistributed under the MIT License.
